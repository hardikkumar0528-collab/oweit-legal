Notification access

Oweit · Effective 18 October 2026

Exactly what happens when you let Oweit read payment alerts.

What Oweit reads

Notifications shown by UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED and similar), bank apps, and SMS or messages apps.

From those, only notifications that contain a rupee amount. In a messages app, only texts from a bank-style sender ID (like HDFCBK) count, never texts from people. Anything that looks like an OTP, PIN, CVV or security code is dropped immediately.

Notifications from all other apps (chats, email, social media, anything else) are ignored.

What Oweit does with it

Finds out whether money went out or came in, how much, and to or from whom, and adds it to your Activity. It suggests subscriptions it sees repeating, notices when an expected charge does not arrive, and can offer to record a UPI payment to a person as money you lent.

All of this happens on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, shared or sold.

You are in control

This is off until you switch it on, and you give permission in Android's own Notification access screen. That screen has one switch for Oweit; inside Oweit, under Settings > Apps Oweit reads, you choose which supported UPI, bank and messages apps are read, and you can switch any of them off.

Turn it off any time with the "Auto-detect charges" switch in Settings, or in Android Settings > Notifications > Notification access. Payments already saved stay until you delete them (Settings > Account & data).

Why Oweit asks for this

Without it, you would have to type every payment yourself. This permission lets Oweit notice payments for you. It is used only for this purpose.
