Delete your data

Oweit · Effective 18 October 2026

How to remove everything Oweit holds about you.

In the app

Open Settings > Account & data > Erase everything. This removes your name, income, people, entries, subscriptions, payments, avatar, Google account details and settings from your phone, signs you out, and takes you back to the start. "Sign out" on the same page keeps your ledger on the phone for the same Google account to return to.

It cannot be undone. If you want to keep a copy first, use Settings > Export ledger.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling Oweit removes its data from your phone. If Google backup is on, Google may keep an older copy in your Google account backup. You can manage or delete those in your Google account settings.

Your subscription

Erasing data does not cancel Oweit Pro. Cancel it in Google Play (see Cancellation & Refunds).

Questions or requests

We hold no copy of your ledger on any server. For anything else about your information, write to mybuddy2031@gmail.com.
