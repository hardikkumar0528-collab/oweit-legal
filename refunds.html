Cancellation & Refunds

Oweit · Effective 18 October 2026

How to cancel Oweit Pro and how refunds work.

Cancelling Oweit Pro

You can cancel any time, with no fee. Open Settings > Manage subscription in the app and follow the link, or go to Google Play > profile picture > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Oweit > Cancel subscription.

You keep Pro until the end of the month you have already paid for. After that, your account returns to the free plan. Your data stays on your phone; only the Pro features and limits change.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. Cancel in Google Play first.

Refunds

Oweit Pro is billed monthly through Google Play. A month that has been used is generally not refundable, but we do not want you to pay for something that did not work.

If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or Pro did not work, write to mybuddy2031@gmail.com within 7 days with the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). You can also ask Google directly at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory, where requests made soon after purchase are often approved automatically.

Nothing here takes away rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or other Indian law.

Free plan

The free plan costs nothing and has no billing. There is nothing to cancel.

Help

mybuddy2031@gmail.com. Please include the email address of your Google account and, for billing problems, the order number.
