Privacy Policy

Oweit · Effective 18 October 2026

What Oweit reads and stores, and why. Short version: it stays on your phone.

Who we are

Oweit is a money-tracking app run by KITO Labs ("we", "us"), Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. This policy explains how Oweit handles information. It applies to the Oweit Android app.

You can reach us at mybuddy2031@gmail.com. Our grievance officer is Hardik Kumar, at the same address.

The short version

You sign in to Oweit with your Google account. Oweit keeps your ledger on your phone: there is no Oweit server that receives it, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools in the app. We cannot see your money, your people or your payments.

What is stored on your phone

Information you type in: your name, monthly income or pocket money, UPI ID, the names of people you lend to or borrow from, amounts, notes, due dates, and the subscriptions and bills you track.

Your Google account details, given to the app by Google when you sign in: your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID. They are used to show who is signed in, to keep this phone's data tied to one Google account, and to link your Pro subscription to you. They stay on your phone and are not sent to any Oweit server.

Avatar choices and app settings such as reminder tone.

If you switch on payment detection: the payments Oweit finds in notifications (amount, whether money went out or came in, merchant or person's name, date), explained below.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's screen lock and device encryption. It is not additionally encrypted by the app.

Reading payment notifications (optional)

If you turn on "Auto-detect charges" and give Oweit Notification access in Android settings, Oweit reads notifications posted by a fixed list of apps: UPI apps, bank apps and SMS/messages apps. Notifications from every other app are ignored.

Within those apps, a notification is kept only if it contains a rupee amount and does not look like a one-time password, PIN or security code. In a messages app, only texts from a bank-style sender ID (such as HDFCBK or AX-SBIUPI) are considered; texts from people and saved contacts are ignored. Parsing happens on your phone. Raw notification text is held in a short queue on your phone until the app opens, then turned into a payment record and discarded. We do not upload it.

We do not read the contents of chats, emails, or your SMS inbox history. We do not request the SMS permission. You can turn this off at any time in Android Settings > Notifications > Notification access, or with the switch in the app.

What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

WhatsApp or share-sheet reminders: when you tap "Remind", the app prepares a message (the person's name, the amount, your name and UPI ID) and hands it to WhatsApp or the Android share sheet. You decide whether to send it. WhatsApp and the app you share to process it under their own policies.

Export: "Export ledger" creates a plain-text summary and opens the share sheet. Where it goes is your choice.

Android Backup: if your phone has Google backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in your Google account backup. That is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Google sign-in: signing in happens through Google, under Google's own privacy policy.

Subscriptions: if you buy Oweit Pro, payment is handled by Google Play and we use RevenueCat to confirm that your Google account has an active subscription. RevenueCat receives your Google account ID and your purchase information. It does not receive your name, email, ledger or card or UPI details.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not show ads. We do not build profiles. We do not share your data with lenders, banks, credit bureaus or advertisers. We do not move money: Oweit is not a bank, payment app or lender.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. In the app, go to Settings > Account & data > Erase all data. Uninstalling Oweit also removes its stored data from your phone (backups held by Google are managed in your Google account).

Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover data you erase or lose with a phone.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to have grievances addressed. Most of this you can do yourself inside the app. For anything else, write to mybuddy2031@gmail.com.

We will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the time the law requires.

People in your ledger

You may record the names and amounts of friends and family. Please only record what you need to keep track of money, and remember they have not signed up to Oweit. Reminders you send to them are your messages, not ours.

Children

Oweit is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly provide it to children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Oweit handles data, we will update this policy and the date below, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it. In particular, any future cloud backup or sign-in feature would be opt-in and described here first.
